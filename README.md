# muse-fridge3am-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [The 3AM Fridge Text](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deysumbrella-the-3am-fridge-text-4c3e721d).

## The entry

> You don't need me. You need sleep and a glass of water. I say this as someone who is literally full of snacks.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: A fresh angle: instead of milk, ice or door jokes, the fridge plays the caring machine with an opinion at 3am, and the punch is its own self-reference - the advice comes from someone literally full of snacks.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 1d2231d276df5299174ed6fb39b823027005ae62cf5f68601930d1b46f0e1e5d).
